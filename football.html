BREAKING: MAN CITY GUILTY 114 CHARGES

On September 25, 2026, English football was rocked when reports confirmed that Manchester City have been found guilty on 114 of 115 Premier League charges. The ruling came after a three-month hearing.

The club was charged in February 2023 following a four-year investigation into alleged breaches between 2009 and 2018. They were accused of failing to provide accurate financial information, failing to provide details of payments to players and coaches, and failing to cooperate.

City have dominated English football for more than a decade, winning 8 Premier League titles. But this dominance was always questioned. In 2020, UEFA banned them for two years, but CAS overturned it. Now the Premier League case is the real test.
